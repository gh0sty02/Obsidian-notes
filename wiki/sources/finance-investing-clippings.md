# Finance and Investing Clippings

## Abstract

Batch summary of finance and investing clippings. These are kept in the wiki even though they sit outside the AI/backend study track, because the vault is allowed to retain useful cross-domain reference material.

## Included Clippings

- `HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Plan Overview, Performance, Portfolio.md` - mutual fund overview, performance, and portfolio data.
- `CYIENT Share Price Live Cyient NSE Today.md` - live market page for Cyient.
- `NIFTY 23,853.90 ▲ +0.98%.md` - NIFTY index snapshot.
- `Stock Screener and fundamental analysis tool for Indian stocks.md` - stock screening/fundamental-analysis tool.
- `The Stock Market Platform to Stay Ahead Stock Screeners, Stock Analysis, and more.md` - market-analysis platform clipping.
- `Tijori Finance.md` - company and sector data platform.
- `Zerodha Varsity Free Stock Market & Trading Courses Online.md` - investing/trading education.
- `The Stocks 3.md` - broad asset/tool directory with design and stock-resource overlap.

## Key Claims

- The finance clippings are mostly reference/tool pages rather than durable conceptual notes.
- Zerodha Varsity is the strongest learning-oriented source in this group.
- Screener, Tijori, and market-platform clippings are useful as discovery tools for financial analysis, but any numeric market data in raw clippings is time-sensitive.

## Methodology

Do not treat captured prices, NAVs, or index values as current. Re-check live sources before making financial decisions.
