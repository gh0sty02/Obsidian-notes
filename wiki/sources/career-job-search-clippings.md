# Career and Job Search Clippings

## Abstract

Batch summary of career and job-search clippings focused on 2026 job-search strategy, AI-assisted applications, referrals, startup targeting, international job markets, solutions engineering, and lower-friction application lists.

## Included Clippings

- `Fastest Way to Land a Job.md` and `Hybrid Job Search Approach 2026.md` - argue for combining AI-assisted workflow with manual targeting.
- `Brutal Job Search Tips 2026.md` - practical job-search heuristics.
- `When to Seek Job Referrals.md` - referral timing and company targeting.
- `Solutions Engineering Jobs at AI Companies.md` and duplicate `Solutions Engineering Jobs at AI Companies 1.md` - solutions engineering as a technical-sales role in AI companies.
- `Recent Unicorn Companies.md`, `Series A and Fast-Growing Startups.md`, `j-delaneyeasy-application...md`, and `Good First Issue.md` - target-company and open-source-entry lists.
- `Japan Jobs for Foreign Talent.md` and `Denmark Jobs for Foreigners.md` - international job-market clippings.
- `Big Tech Programs.md` - programs such as Google Gen AI Academy, Google Summer of Code, AWS Community Builders, MLH Fellowship, Hacktoberfest, and student plans.
- `Side Hustle Stack is the ultimate database of earning opportunities.md` - earning-opportunity directory.

## Key Claims

- A high-leverage search combines broad discovery with narrow targeting: use job boards and AI to identify opportunities, then prioritize companies with less competition, fast growth, or direct-contact paths.
- Referrals work best when the candidate can explain a precise role fit; they are weaker when used as a generic mass-apply tactic.
- AI companies create demand for hybrid technical-commercial roles such as solutions engineering, where integration skill and customer communication matter as much as pure coding.
- Startup lists, easy-application repositories, and open-source issues can create alternate entry paths when mainstream job boards are saturated.

## Methodology

Most clippings are advice-oriented and time-sensitive. Use them as tactical inputs, then verify links, hiring status, visa constraints, and program dates before acting.
