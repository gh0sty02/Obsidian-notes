# Finance Resources

Finance material in the current wiki is a resource catalog, not investment advice.

## Resource Types

- **Market snapshots:** NIFTY and individual stock pages capture a moment in time.
- **Fund pages:** HDFC Small Cap Fund clipping captures fund overview/performance/portfolio information.
- **Screeners and analysis platforms:** Screener-style tools, Tijori, and stock-market platforms help compare companies, fundamentals, and market data.
- **Education:** Zerodha Varsity is a structured learning source for stock-market and trading concepts.

## Usage Notes

- Treat captured numeric values as stale immediately; verify live data before using them.
- Prefer learning resources and tool catalogs for durable wiki value.
- Keep finance pages separate from AI/backend study notes so cross-domain material stays discoverable without polluting the technical roadmap.
