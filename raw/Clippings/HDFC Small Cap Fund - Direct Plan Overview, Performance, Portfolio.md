About HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Plan

HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Plan is a equity mutual fund scheme of HDFC Mutual Fund. Launched on January 01, 2013, it is currently managed by Chirag Setalvad. The fund has an expense ratio of 0.62% with an overall AUM (Assets Under Management) of ₹38,809 Cr.

HDFC Small Cap Fund - Direct Plan is mandated to invest at least 65 per cent of its assets in small-cap stocks at all times. The fund allows minimum lumpsum investment of ₹100 and minimum SIP of ₹100.

Investment Strategy

The scheme seeks to provide long-term capital appreciation /income by investing predominantly in Small-Cap companies.

Suitability

Small-cap funds are suitable for investors:

- Seeking exposure in small-cap stocks for high returns
- Aiming for long-term wealth creation
- With a 7+ year investment horizon
- Who can withstand interim sharp declines in investment value

Note:

- Invest only through SIP

Capital Gains Taxation

- If the mutual fund units are sold after 1 year from the date of investment, gains upto Rs 1.25 lakh in a financial year are exempt from tax. Gains over Rs 1.25 lakh are taxed at the rate of 12.5%.
- If the mutual fund units are sold within 1 year from the date of investment, entire amount of gain is taxed at the rate of 20%.
- No tax is to be paid as long as you continue to hold the units.
