## Free and open stock market and financial education

Varsity is an extensive and in-depth collection of stock market and financial lessons created by Karthik Rangappa at [Zerodha](https://zerodha.com/). It is free and openly accessible to everyone and is one of the largest financial education resources on the web. No signup, no pay-wall, no ads.
